BonusRoll Plus

The group loot roll window of World of Warcraft, rebuilt. BonusRoll Plus replaces Blizzard's small Need / Greed / Disenchant / Pass popup with a clean panel that shows the item, its level, the armor slot, how it compares with what you wear, its value, and a live list of what everyone in your group chose and rolled.

What you see on every roll

  • The item with the color of its quality and the item level in brackets.
  • The slot and armor type (for example "Chest · Mail"), followed by the item level difference against what you currently wear: green +12 for an upgrade, red -5 for a downgrade.
  • The value of the item: vendor price, auction house price and disenchant value (TradeSkillMaster prices when you have it), each with its icon and the game's coin icons and a gold BiS tag when the item is one of your picks in NaowhForever_BiS. Items with no value say "Cannot be sold".
  • Ctrl + click on the item icon opens the game's dressing room, so you can see how the piece looks on your character (transmog).
  • A warning in red when you cannot use the item. Need is never blocked: you can still roll Need to sell the item, as many players do with BoE items.
  • The four buttons: Need, Greed, Disenchant and Pass, with a time bar.
  • A rolls list next to the window with each player's class icon, their choice, their roll and a crown for the leader. The winner is marked, and the window closes a few seconds after the item is awarded (2 by default, you choose).
  • Seconds left on the time bar, with colors as time runs out.
  • Optional alerts: a sound when a roll appears and a flashing frame when time is running out.
  • Automatic rules (off by default): auto-pass items that lower your item level, auto-greed cheap BoE items, and auto-Need recipes of your professions that you have not learned yet. They always tell you in the chat what they did. Only the recipe rule ever rolls Need.

When many items drop

Only a few windows are shown at once (3 by default). The rest wait as compact one-line rows with the item, its level, the four buttons and a thin time bar, so you can answer without waiting your turn. When a window closes, the next one takes its place.

Settings window (/br open)

Open it with /br open, or with the "Open settings" button in the game's AddOns options.

10 window designs

Frosted glass (the default), arcane parchment, steel forge, violet void, gold chest, runic stone, frost, dark minimal, Alliance and Horde, each with a live preview.

Options

  • Size, opacity and number of visible windows (up to 5, 3 by default), and how many seconds the window stays after the winner is announced.
  • Use the game's own loot window: BonusRoll Plus stops drawing its own window but keeps the list with everyone's rolls and shows it right next to the game's window.
  • Hide the game's "You received" popup that appears at the bottom when you win an item (off by default).
  • Show or hide the rolls list next to each window, choose the stack direction and the space between windows, change the text size and use always-compact one-line rows.
  • Position: show the test windows, drag them where you want and lock the position. They stay there for future loot. Every window also has its own padlock.
  • Item level difference, compact rows, value, sound and flash can each be switched on or off.

Profiles

Save your settings as a profile, switch between profiles, load one automatically on a given character, specialization or kind of content (raids, dungeons, open world), and export or import a profile as a short text to share it.

History and statistics

The History tab shows how many rolls you took part in, how many you won (with Need and with Greed) and your wins in the last 7 days, a summary of the session with the total value of what you won, a filter by quality, an export as text and your last rolls won. Items show their item level, follow the game's language and open their tooltip when you hover or click them. /br history prints the same list in the chat with clickable links.

About

The About tab lists the changelog of every version.

Commands

/br openOpens the settings window.
/br testShows test windows with your own equipped items. Use the same button in the settings window to hide them.
/br scale 0.5-1.5Changes the size.
/br rollsShows or hides the rolls list.
/br lockLocks or unlocks the windows.
/br resetPuts the windows back in their default position.
/br historyYour last rolls won.
/br versionShows the installed version.
/br debugPrints what the addon reads from the chat, for troubleshooting.

Good to know

  • While BonusRoll Plus is enabled, Blizzard's own roll windows are hidden, so only one window shows, unless you choose to use the game's own window in the settings. Disable the addon and they come back.
  • The rolls list reads the loot chat and starts empty for every item, so Need, Greed and Pass choices show up for everyone in the group and never carry rolls from an earlier drop of the same item.
  • Fully translated into the 11 languages of the game: English, German, Spanish (Spain and Mexico), French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The settings window also shows a random World of Warcraft fact, different every time you open it.
  • Updates are announced in the chat when another player in your group or guild has a newer version.
  • This addon is exclusive to the WoW Forever client.

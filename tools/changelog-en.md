## 0.7.0

### New
- **Automatic rules** (off by default): auto-pass items that lower your item level and auto-greed cheap BoE items. They always tell you in the chat what they did and they never roll Need.
- **Disenchant and auction value** on every roll with TradeSkillMaster: vendor price, auction house price (with the auction house icon) and disenchant value (with the disenchant icon), shown with the game's coin icons. Items with no value say "Cannot be sold".
- **BiS tag** next to the value when the item is one of your picks in NaowhForever_BiS.
- **Seconds left** on the time bar, with colors as time runs out.
- **Stack direction and spacing** of the windows, **text size** and an **always-compact** mode with one-line rows.
- **Profiles for content**: load a profile automatically in raids, dungeons or the open world.
- **History**: session summary with the total value of what you won, filter by quality and export as text.

### Improvement
- The settings window is organized in sections (windows, alerts, automatic rules, tools) and scrolls.

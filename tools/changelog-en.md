## 0.10.0 - Item level on gear, new warnings and tidier settings

### Item level on equipment
- The **item level now shows in brackets, in the item's quality colour, before the name** of weapons, armor, rings and trinkets, in item tooltips, in the loot chat, in the roll window and in the history. Materials and vendor goods are left alone.
- Item tooltips also show **how the item compares with what you wear** (+12 or -3) and its **auction and disenchant value** when TradeSkillMaster is installed. Each one has its own setting.

### New warnings and notices
- The roll window warns when an item **binds when equipped** or is **armor, a weapon or a shield your class cannot use**.
- When you win an item you hear a **sound you can pick from a list of 13**, with a button to test it before choosing, and a reminder that **you can trade the item to the group for 2 hours**.
- If you lose a roll by 3 points or less, a "so close" message tells you the numbers.

### Settings and history
- The settings are now **split into sections by content** instead of one long list.
- The history can show **only your wins**.

### Also
- Translated into all 11 languages.

## 0.7.0 - Know what an item is worth, and let the addon handle the boring rolls

### See the value at a glance
- **Every roll now shows what the item is worth**, right under its name: the vendor price, the auction house price (with the auction house icon) and the disenchant value (with the disenchant icon), all with the game's own gold, silver and copper icons. The auction and disenchant prices need TradeSkillMaster. Items that cannot be sold say "Cannot be sold" instead of a confusing "0 copper".
- **BiS tag**: if you use NaowhForever_BiS, items that are one of your best-in-slot picks get a gold BiS tag.
- **Seconds left** are shown on the time bar, turning amber and then red as time runs out, so you never lose a roll by surprise.

### Let the addon roll for you (off by default)
- **Auto-pass downgrades**: automatically pass on gear that would lower your item level by the amount you choose.
- **Auto-greed cheap BoE items**: automatically roll Greed on tradable items worth less than the gold amount you choose.
- Both rules tell you in the chat exactly what they did, and they never roll Need.

### Make it look and behave your way
- Choose the **direction the windows stack** (up, down, left or right) and the **space between them**.
- **Text size** from 80% to 140%, and an **always-compact mode** that shows every roll as a single line.
- **Profiles for content**: load a profile automatically in raids, dungeons or the open world.

### History
- A **summary of your session** with the total value of everything you won.
- **Filter by quality** (green, blue, epic) and **export your history as text** to copy it anywhere.

### Also
- The settings window is now organized in clear sections (windows, alerts, automatic rules, tools) and scrolls.
- Everything is translated into all 11 languages.

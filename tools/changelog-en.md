## 0.8.0 - Keep the game's loot window, and never miss a recipe

### Use the game's own loot window
- **New option** to use the loot roll window that comes with the game. BonusRoll Plus no longer draws its own window, but **keeps the list with everyone's rolls** and shows it right next to the game's window.
- Turn it on or off at any time in the settings (/br open).

### Never miss a recipe (off by default)
- **Auto-Need on recipes you have not learned**: if you have a profession and a recipe for it drops that you have not learned yet, the addon rolls Need for you. It tells you in the chat what it did.
- Recipes you already know, and recipes for professions you do not have, are left alone.
- Both options are included in your profiles and translated into all 11 languages.

## 0.6.0

### New
- **Vendor or auction value** on every roll (TradeSkillMaster prices when you have it), with a purple `*` when the item has an appearance you do not own yet.
- **History tab** in the settings window with statistics: rolls you took part in, rolls won, how many with Need and with Greed, and wins in the last 7 days.
- **Profiles per specialization**, besides per character.
- **Optional alerts**: a sound when a roll appears and a flashing frame when time is running out.
- **New setting** to choose how many seconds the window stays after the winner is announced (2 by default).

### Improvement
- Window designs reduced from 20 to the 10 best finished ones: frosted glass, arcane parchment, steel forge, violet void, gold chest, runic stone, frost, dark minimal, Alliance and Horde.
- The settings window opens faster.
- The addon is now exclusive to the WoW Forever client.

### Fix
- When two or more items dropped at once, one of the roll windows could be missing.
